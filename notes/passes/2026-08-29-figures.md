# Figures and charts pass

Date: 2026-08-29
Scope: whole manuscript
Verdict: **green for colleague sharing**

## Procedure

The pass registry names `check-chart-style`, but that skill is not available in
this environment. I therefore applied the registered figure-pass procedure
directly and audited the plotting source, aggregate inputs, exported figures,
captions, in-text citations, and rendered manuscript.

## Figure inventory

1. **Response functions by study and elicitation method.** Two panels compare
   magnitude estimation with the fixed seven-point scale for Sprouse, Schütze,
   and Almeida (2013) and Sprouse and Almeida (2017). The plot shows the common
   signal, linear summaries, and bounded logistic curvature.
2. **Multiverse of operational definitions.** Three panels show endpoint
   resolution, prediction increments, and sign disagreement across the
   admissible specification families. The localized 2017 rank/forced-choice
   result is visible without obscuring the otherwise weak evidence.

Both figures earn their place: the first makes the response-function result
legible at a glance, while the second makes the robustness structure and its
qualification recoverable without reading a dense list of specifications.

## Data and numerical fidelity

- Figure 1 uses all aggregate rows in the retained public-data pipeline: 298
  shared stimulus identifiers labelled `condition` in the 2013 files and 786
  labelled `item` in the 2017 files. The caption makes clear that this is a
  difference in source-file nomenclature rather than in the plotted quantity.
- Figure 2 includes all 54 admissible endpoint specifications (27 per study),
  of which one crosses the stated threshold.
- Figure 2 includes all 72 prediction specifications (36 per study). Exactly
  four cross the stated threshold, all for the 2017 rank/forced-choice target.
- The decision panel reports the audited disagreement counts: 3/150 and 1/50,
  with no support for the preregistered direction. It also traces those counts
  to 2013 pair IDs 41, 83, and 91 and 2017 pair ID p1, which are invariant
  across all nine ME--Likert scoring combinations.
- The script checks schemas, study identities, and expected row counts before
  plotting and fails rather than silently drawing incomplete inputs.

## Chart style and accessibility

- The plotting script loads the central house `plot_style.py` resolved through
  the project's `.house-style` symlink; typography and palette therefore follow
  the current central style rather than a local snapshot.
- Axes name their quantities and units. Small multiples separate study and
  specification families instead of relying on overplotting.
- Colour is never the only distinguishing channel. In the multiverse figure,
  endpoint status uses a neutral circle versus a golden star; prediction target
  uses filled blue versus open golden markers; mapping uses circles versus
  triangles; and the prediction criterion uses solid golden squares. The legend names
  all four target--mapping combinations explicitly, including the open golden
  triangle for a yes/no target with a rank mapping, and the caption defines
  every channel. Nearby specifications are stacked symmetrically around each
  study row; the caption states that their vertical positions have no
  substantive meaning.
- Captions identify the source studies, aggregation level, sample sizes or
  specification counts, comparison baseline, thresholds, and the intended
  interpretation. A reader can interpret either figure from its caption alone.
- Scatter layers in the manuscript PDF are rendered at approximately 338 ppi;
  curves, labels, and other figure elements remain vector graphics.
- These are data figures, not conceptual box-and-arrow diagrams, so they import
  no additional causal or containment metaphor. Venue-specific alt text can be
  added later if a submission venue requires a separate accessibility field.

## Manuscript integration and reproducibility

- Both figures are cited in the prose before they appear and have stable labels:
  `fig:response-functions` and `fig:multiverse-landscape`.
- The captions use full author attribution rather than the misleading shorthand
  “Sprouse data.”
- `analysis/make_manuscript_figures.py` reads only retained aggregate outputs
  and writes both PDF and PNG versions. No participant-level data are used or
  distributed.
- The exports are deterministic: regeneration in a clean temporary directory
  produces byte-identical PDFs.
- The manuscript was rebuilt after the final figure exports. All 13 pages were
  rendered and visually checked; neither figure clips, overlaps, or becomes
  illegible at page size.

Final assessment: **green**. The figures accurately expose the paper's two main
empirical patterns, remain reproducible from the shareable aggregate outputs,
and improve the colleague-facing version without adding unsupported claims.
