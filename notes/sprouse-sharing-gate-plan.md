# Sprouse-sharing gate plan

## Aim

Prepare the strongest reasonable colleague-facing version of *Was magnitude
estimation necessary?* for Jon Sprouse. The standard is not a journal submission
package. It is a manuscript whose empirical claims, treatment of prior work,
reader-facing argument, citations, and rendered PDF can be trusted by the scholar
whose data and work are central to the reassessment.

## Gates to run

1. **Fresh baseline and empirical integrity**
   - Record the current source, page, and word counts.
   - Reproduce the analysis from fresh public Sprouse inputs if the recorded
     author-site files are reachable; otherwise verify every manuscript statistic
     against the retained aggregate outputs and report the limitation.
   - Audit all reported counts, correlations, intervals, model-fit values, and
     pair-level results against their generated sources.
   - Verify the Bard quotations and page references against the local primary
     source.

2. **Contribution and fair-engagement audit**
   - Check alignment among title, abstract, introduction, body, and conclusion.
   - Make sure Bard et al., Sprouse et al., Sprouse and Almeida, and Langsford et
     al. are characterized in their strongest relevant form before criticism.
   - Narrow or remove unsupported novelty and negative-literature claims.

3. **Reader pass with targeted edits**
   - Read the manuscript in order as Sprouse is likely to read it.
   - Repair orientation, transitions, definitions, results legibility, and stale
     editorial language while preserving claims, evidence, numerical results,
     citations, scope, and Brett's voice.
   - Prefer local clarity repair over structural reconstruction unless the
     manuscript's actual argument proves unrecoverable.

4. **Conceptual and terminological audit**
   - Check the distinctions among magnitude estimation, formal measurement,
     acceptability, grammaticality, item-level signal, response-function
     curvature, resolution, reliability, and decision relevance.
   - Check explanatory levels and the separation of participant responses,
     published judgments, benchmark labels, and grammatical inference.
   - Run the projectibility audit, especially the claimed projection target,
     warrant, scope, and conclusion.

5. **Mechanics and artifact verification**
   - Run proofreading, terminology, house-style, quotation, and bibliography
     integrity checks.
   - Apply only verified repairs, then rerun the relevant checks.
   - Complete a full XeLaTeX/Biber build, scan the log, render every PDF page,
     and inspect the final artifact for layout defects.

## Deliberately excluded

- Glossa-specific policy and submission-form work.
- Anonymous-review packaging and `main-blind.pdf`.
- Repository deposit, DOI, cover letter, and open-data packaging decisions.
- A new simulated review board or adversarial cold read.
- A wholesale reread of every cited source already covered by the July
  source-to-claim audit.
- Commit or push operations.

## Deliverable

A freshly built sharing PDF, the edited LaTeX source, concise pass records, exact
final page/word counts, and a short note of any remaining risks that Jon should
know about.
